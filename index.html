The street we call home

The Wollzeile story

One narrow street, two Vienna legends. The Wollzeile winds behind Stephansdom like a woollen thread, and for over a century it has been the address where the city stores its cosiness.

1905

Wollzeile - Where the schnitzel was born

Johann Figlmüller opens a small wine tavern here. The schnitzel that made the street famous is born — a plate-overflowing legend that still draws a queue down the lane every single day.

Today

Two doors of cosiness

Follow the curve of the lane past the cathedral's shadow and the scent of schnitzel: a little smaller, a little woollier — ME keeps Vienna's feet warm, one pair at a time.

"Between St. Stephen's towers and Figlmüller's pans, we keep Vienna's feet warm."
